Bio

My approach to graphic design and digital marketing is rooted in the belief that creativity and strategy work best together. Through my dual-degree studies in Graphic Design and Digital Audiences at Arizona State University, I've developed a strong interest in how visual communication, audience research, and marketing strategy influence the way people connect with brands.

Throughout my studies, I've gained hands-on experience in branding, typography, digital advertising, social media marketing, and analytics. My projects have allowed me to explore both the creative and strategic sides of marketing, from developing visual concepts to researching audiences, evaluating campaign performance, and understanding what makes communication effective.

I believe good design should do more than look appealing. It should communicate a clear message, solve a problem, and connect with the intended audience. I approach projects with both a designer's eye and a marketer's mindset, balancing creativity with research, strategy, and measurable goals.

As I move into the next chapter of my career, I'm looking forward to applying these skills in a professional environment, expanding my experience, and contributing to projects that bring together thoughtful design, effective communication, and meaningful results.
